# cdr_exclusions.txt - tickers excluded from the TSX universe as depositary receipts.
#
# A CDR is a CAD-hedged receipt (issued by CIBC and others) wrapping a foreign
# stock. It trades with a .TO symbol and FMP serves the PARENT's profile and
# fundamentals, so Apple appears as a Toronto-listed Technology company.
#
# FMP's isAdr flag does NOT identify these - it is False for every one of them,
# which is why backfill_ticker_flags.py found zero ADRs in a universe that was
# one-quarter CDRs. country='US' does not work either: Brookfield, Coeur Mining
# and Ivanhoe Electric are US-domiciled and genuinely TSX-listed.
#
# Candidates were generated by: country != CA and dollar-volume/market-cap
# < 500ppm (a CDR reports its parent's market cap but trades thin in Toronto),
# then reviewed by hand. The screen is a shortlist generator, NOT the rule -
# this reviewed file is the rule.
#
# Generated 2026-10-18. 122 excluded, 19 reviewed and kept.
# Re-review when the universe is refreshed; new CDRs launch regularly.
